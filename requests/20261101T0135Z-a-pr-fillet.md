## Change

Fillet keeping (#5677) swaps an arc's ends when its sweep jumps by more than half a turn and both its tangent lines
turned. The jump is judged against a reference: the positions before a move, or a start the caller hands in. When that
reference shows the corner mirrored, a fillet the solve left exactly where it was looks turned, and the swap makes it
its long complement. The solve still reports ok, with both lines now cutting back through the arc.

`settle` now lets the lines decide. When an arc's tangent lines all leave it after the solve, the arc already rounds
its corner and keeps its ends, whatever the reference showed. A swap still gives a corner its arc back when its lines
cut back through it, which is the case #5677 is for.

leave. It already passes on `main`. It is here because the narrower fix, letting a swap only shorten an arc, fails it
(sweep 60°).
