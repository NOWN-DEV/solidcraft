## Change

A sheet with hundreds of cut-outs was refused by the flat budget ("sheet metal flat geometry exceeds the bounded
processing budget"), because three steps grew with the square of the cut-out edges:

- every cut-out was merged with every other in one growing union and cut from each piece in one boolean;
- the face build looked, for every straight edge, at every corner and every edge of the sheet for its neighbour;
- a cut into an unfolded flat clipped each of its tools against the panel's whole outline, so an outline of a thousand
  edges (torn corner reliefs, a serrated edge) was charged once per hole.

Now:

- cut-outs are merged only where their boxes meet (one sweep along x), each cluster unioned on its own;
- a cut-out whose box meets no other cut-out and no edge of the material, and whose outline is a plain closed loop,
  becomes a hole of the material it lies in without a boolean, in the flat pattern and in each piece of the folded solid;
- a flat cut's tool whose box meets no edge of the panel's outline and no cut-out lies wholly inside or wholly outside
  the panel: it is taken as it is, or dropped, without the boolean (`NearHoles::clear`);
- the face build finds the corners on an edge from a grid of about one corner per square, and an edge's neighbour from a
  hash of where edges start; both are charged by what they visit, with saturating keys;
- both shortcuts take only loops the planar booleans would give back as they are: closed, finite, within reach, no
  zero-length piece, an enclosed area (wider on average than a few millionths of a mm) and simple (no chord of the loop
  crosses or touches another but its neighbours at their shared corner; the chords are compared in one sweep over their
  boxes). A sliver there and back, points on a line or a bow-tie go to the booleans, which drop or resolve them. The
  material holding free holes is measured against the flat caps again;
- the new lookups charge the flat budget for what they read: the outline and cut-out boxes, the box scan and
  point-in-outline test per tool, the point-in-loop tests that place a free cut-out (only the holes whose boxes hold its
  point are walked).

The prism cut's upfront charge (`eval_more.rs`) now counts the regions' edges against the number of tools (and the
text), not against every face of the tools. A tool made of 368 logo outlines has a face per edge, so the old charge
refused the cut before anything was cut, although the work it stands for (finding each tool's footprint among the
regions, and the regions' outlines against the text) is done once per tool.

## Repro (before)

A 1200 x 600 x 1.5 tray with four 90° flanges; sketch 368 copies of a 32-edge logo outline (or 800 Ø3 circles) on the
base and cut them through: refused with the flat-budget error. Or: an unfolded 500 x 300 blank whose far edge is 500
teeth of 1 x 0.5 mm, one fold, then one cut of 400 Ø3 holes and a refold: refused.

The last case is the engine test below and builds now; the 1600-cut-out unit test covers the many-cut-outs case.

## Validation

New tests:

- `sheet::tests::cut_outs_apart_skip_the_booleans` (doc): which cut-outs are free (in the material, in its hole,
  outside, a round one) and which go to the booleans (across the outline or a hole's edge, overlapping, with an island, an
  open loop, a sliver there and back, three points on a line, a bow-tie); on a plate the sliver and the line leave the
  holes and faces of a plate without them; 2000 apart cut-outs merge within a sweep's work.
- `sheet::tests::a_tool_clear_of_the_outline_and_cut_outs_is_clipped_without_the_booleans` (doc): `NearHoles::clear`
  takes a tool inside as it is, drops one outside, leaves one across the outline, one meeting a cut-out, an open loop and
  the three degenerate loops to the booleans, and charges its lookups.
- `sheet::tests::a_sheet_of_thousands_of_cut_outs_stays_within_its_budget` (doc): 1600 squares on a flanged plate.
- `sheet_tests::an_unfolded_sheet_with_a_long_outline_takes_four_hundred_holes_in_one_cut` (engine): the serrated blank
  above; the removed volume is 400 holes' within 1 %.

Checks run on this branch: `cargo fmt --all --check`; `cargo clippy -p solidcraft-doc -p solidcraft-engine
--all-targets -- -D warnings`; `cargo test -p solidcraft-doc` (518 passed); `cargo test -p solidcraft-engine --lib --
cmd::sheet` (211 passed). Each new test fails with the shortcuts it covers switched off: the engine test and the
thousand-cut-out test with the flat-budget error above, the other two on their asserts; the degenerate-loop cases failed
before the area and simplicity checks were added.
