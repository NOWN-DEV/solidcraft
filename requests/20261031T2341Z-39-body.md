## Change

A sketch with a text whose words come from a text parameter (#6364) is left unsolved after the words change and its
links then move far, with the warning "the sketch constraints conflict (k1, …, k10)". The same two changes the other way
round solve.

Other words are other outlines, so the text is built anew with new points and curves. The solve that walks a sketch
from its stored state to a far link move (and the check for sides the jump turned over) refused the two sketches as not
the same entities, and the single jump left alone failed.

`Sketch::stored_with_rebuilt_texts` gives the stored sketch as this one has its entities when they differ only by
rebuilt texts: the texts' entities as they are now, every other point, radius, arc turn and constraint value as
stored. `Document::solve_sketch` walks from that, so the points pair up again. Sketches that differ in anything else are
refused as before.

## Repro

1. Sketch `A`: a 20 mm vertical line whose start the length parameters `x`, `y` place at (100, 200), and its
   midpoint. Text parameter `Label` = `A1`.
2. Sketch `S` projects the line's ends and midpoint, draws a construction rectangle on them (far side parallel, 5 mm
   from the midpoint) and a centred text 3 high on the far side with `text_expression: "Label + '-1'"`.
3. Change `Label` to `B2`, then change `x`, `y` to (300, 400), about 280 mm away.

Before: `S` warns "the sketch constraints conflict (k1, …, k10)". Without the word change, or with the two changes the
other way round, it solves.
